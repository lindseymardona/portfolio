i'm Lindsey — a designer, developer, and musician who likes making complicated things feel simple. i studied cognitive science with a computing specialization at UCLA (plus minors in digital humanities and data science engineering), which is a fancy way of saying i've always been fascinated by how people think and how technology can meet them where they are.

that curiosity pulled me toward UX. most recently i've been the sole designer at UCLA's Latino Policy & Politics Institute, where i designed the Latino Climate & Health Dashboard — turning dense, neighborhood-level research into factsheets that advocates and legislative staff can actually read and act on. it taught me that design is as much about restraint and facilitation as it is about pixels.

along the way i've built interactive maps, prototyped robot-monitoring interfaces in Seoul, run competitive research, and wrapped up Google's UX Design Certificate. i care about the whole arc of a product — the research, the system, the visual craft, and the way it lands for a real person.

when i'm not designing, i'm singing and writing music — i sing with a community chamber choir and recently co-wrote a song for Miku Expo 2024. i think the two halves feed each other: both are about timing, texture, and making something land emotionally.
